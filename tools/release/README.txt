Star Wars: Rogue Squadron - Recompiled
======================================

A native Windows port of Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (Nintendo 64), made by statically recompiling the game.

This download contains no game code or assets. You need your own ROM of the game, dumped from your
cartridge: Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (USA) (Rev 1), a 16 MB big-endian .z64 file,
SHA-1 c12e1c6fb47a67da463c50b705ec682ec24ae80d.

Requirements
------------
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- A graphics card with Vulkan or Direct3D 12 support.

How to play
-----------
1. Extract this folder anywhere.
2. Run RogueSquadronRecompiled.exe.
3. Choose "Start game" and select your ROM the first time. It is remembered after that.

Settings (graphics, widescreen, controls, sound) are in the launcher and in game. Widescreen: set the aspect ratio
to "Expand" to widen in-flight gameplay; the intro, cutscenes and menus stay 4:3. Your settings and saves are
stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\RogueSquadronRecompiled.
